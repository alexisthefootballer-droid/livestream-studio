Delete your account

You can delete your LiveStream Studio account and data at any time.

In the app

  1. Open LiveStream Studio and log in.
  2. Go to Settings and privacy → Account → Delete account.
  3. Read the warning, type DELETE and tap Delete my account forever.

What is deleted

Your profile, photo and bio; all your videos and video files; your comments, likes and saves; your messages, followers and following; your coins and your settings and history.

Deletion is permanent and can not be undone. Coins and earnings under $10 are lost and are not refunded.

Before you delete

If you have earnings of $10 or more, or a withdrawal waiting to be paid, withdraw first. The app will tell you if this blocks deletion.

Can not log in?

If you can not open the app, log in on any device where the app still works, then use Settings → Help Center, choose the topic Account and describe the problem.
