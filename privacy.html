Privacy Policy

How LiveStream Studio handles your information.

1. What we collect

Your email, username, name, photo and bio; the videos, comments, likes, saves and follows you make; your messages; videos you watched; and your privacy settings.

2. How we use it

To run the app: show your content, deliver messages, suggest videos, keep people safe and handle payments. We do not sell your data and we do not show ads.

3. Who can see it

That depends on your settings. See Settings → Privacy Center to control who sees your videos, comments, messages, liked videos and following list.

4. Your choices

You can download your data, change your privacy settings, block people, delete your videos, or ask us to delete your account from Settings → Privacy Center.

5. Keeping data safe

Your data is stored with secure cloud services and protected by access rules. No system is perfect, so use a strong password and log out on shared devices.

6. Children

The app is not for children under 13. If you think a child is using it, contact us from Help Center.

Delete your data. You can delete your account and all your data inside the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or follow the steps on the Delete account page.
